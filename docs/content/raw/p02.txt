Use organic and chemical fertilizer together. DOA data show compost holds about
1.5-0.4-1.0% N-P-K, while chicken manure holds about 2.4-6.3-2.1%. The DOA advises
building the soil gradually with straw, green manure and compost, using chemical
fertilizer while fertility is low, and cutting back as the soil improves.
